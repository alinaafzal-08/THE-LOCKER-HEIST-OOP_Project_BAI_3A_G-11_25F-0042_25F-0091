+------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    ABOUT THE GAME                          |
+------------------------------------------------------------+

  The Locker Heist is a console-based detective mystery game.
  A priceless artefact has been stolen from the mansion of
  Mr. Leonardo. Seven suspects live under the same roof. Only
  one of them is the culprit -- and you must find out who.

+------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      YOUR MISSION                          |
+------------------------------------------------------------+

  1. Search the mansion's locations for hidden letter clues.
  2. Interview the suspects to gather more evidence.
  3. Use the clues to form two secret words on your Tile Rack.
  4. Identify the culprit from the word pair you have built.
  5. Make your accusation before your credibility runs out.

+------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     HOW TO BEGIN                           |
+------------------------------------------------------------+

  From the Main Menu select:
    [1] New Game      -- start a fresh investigation
    [2] Leaderboard   -- see the top detectives
    [3] About         -- you are reading this now
    [4] Rules         -- game rules and scoring details
    [5] Exit          -- quit the program

  Good luck, Detective. The truth is hidden in the words.
